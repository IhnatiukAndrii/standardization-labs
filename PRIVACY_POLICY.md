## 2. GDPR & Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting user privacy. This section explains how we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and handle user settings/game data.

### 2.1 No Server-Side Processing
The 15-Puzzle Game is a static web application. We **do not** collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data on remote servers. All game mechanics, settings, and scores run entirely in your web browser.

### 2.2 Local Storage & Client-Side Data
To enhance your gameplay experience, we store the following data locally in your browser's `localStorage`:
- **User Settings**: Username, game board size, grid colors, and preferences.
- **Game Results**: A local history of your completed games, including move count and time taken (used for the leaderboards).

This data never leaves your device and can be deleted at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

### 2.3 Cookie Consent & GDPR Configuration
According to the GDPR, we request consent before storing non-essential client-side data. We classify our cookie and storage usage into the following categories:

1. **Necessary Data (Always Active)**:
   - Stores your cookie consent decisions so the banner does not reappear.
   - Core game configuration essential for the app to function.
2. **Analytics Data (Optional)**:
   - Tracks gameplay statistics (e.g., total moves, game completions) locally to show progress graphs. No external analytics services (like Google Analytics) are active.
3. **Marketing Data (Optional)**:
   - Reserved for personalization features (currently unused by this application).

You can modify or withdraw your consent at any time via the Cookie Configuration options available in the footer/popup banner.

### 2.4 Your Rights
Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data. Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can exercise these rights yourself by:
- Clearing your browser's cookies and local storage.
- Re-configuring your cookie choices through the configuration banner.
